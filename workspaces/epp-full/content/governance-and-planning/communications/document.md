# Communications

## Purpose
This document explains how {{facility_name}} maintains its emergency communications framework, keeps communication responsibilities organized, and supports reliable information sharing before, during, and after emergencies.

## Communications Framework
{{facility_name}} maintains a written emergency communications plan that complies with applicable law, supports the Emergency Preparedness Program, and is reviewed at least annually. The plan is also updated when communication methods, contact responsibilities, reporting systems, contact lists, or partner relationships materially change.

The communications plan identifies the categories of contacts that must be maintained for emergency use, the leadership roles responsible for keeping that information current, and the locations of the supporting lists, systems, and tools used during response and recovery. At a minimum, the plan addresses communication with staff, resident physicians, entities providing services under arrangement, other healthcare facilities, volunteers, emergency management agencies, emergency responders, health departments, and other sources of assistance.

Detailed phone lists, device inventories, software instructions, radio or backup-device procedures, and other operational communication steps are maintained in the related all-hazards response, resource, evacuation, incident command, and appendix documents rather than repeated here.

## Contact Information and Alternate Methods
{{facility_name}} maintains staff emergency contact information in {{employee_contact_information_location}} and maintains other contact information in designated facility systems and resource lists so emergency leaders can reach the people and organizations needed to support resident safety and continuity of operations. Contact information should be reviewed on a routine basis and corrected when staff roles, provider relationships, response partners, or emergency contact methods change.

The communications plan also identifies primary and alternate methods for contacting staff and external partners. Alternate methods may be needed when normal telephones, internet access, internal systems, or vendor-supported services are disrupted. The plan should make clear where staff can find the approved fallback methods and who is authorized to activate them.

## Information Sharing and Reporting
The communications plan establishes how {{facility_name}} shares time-sensitive information during an emergency. This includes approved methods for communicating with staff, emergency management partners, healthcare partners, health departments, mutual aid partners or healthcare coalitions when applicable, and others who need operational information to support continuity of care and response coordination.

During an emergency, the facility may need to communicate its occupancy, resource or asset needs, and ability to provide assistance to other facilities. These reports should be made through assigned Incident Command System roles and through the reporting methods approved for the facility's jurisdiction.

Resident information and medical documentation may be shared only through authorized roles and permitted processes. This document establishes the communications governance for that information sharing; the records-protection expectations are maintained in Resident Medical Information, and packet-level evacuation workflows are maintained in the related evacuation documents.

## Coordination With Related Documents
This document is the governance-level communications topic. It should be read with the related documents that contain the operational detail:

- Section B and other all-hazards response documents describe staff notification, internal communications, external communications, and fallback communication actions during a response.
- Section C and incident command documents assign communication responsibilities during activation.
- Section D and evacuation documents describe resident relocation communications, transfer coordination, and evacuation reporting.
- Section F and resource-list documents maintain detailed contact directories and emergency resource lists.
- Appendix G and related resident-facing materials explain how emergency plan information is shared with residents and responsible parties.

## What Staff Should Understand
- The communications plan is a maintained program document, not just a call list.
- Staff, provider, volunteer, agency, and partner contact information must stay current.
- Primary communication methods may fail, so staff should know where approved alternate methods are described.
- Occupancy, resource needs, ability to assist, and similar operational reports are made through assigned roles and approved systems.
- Sensitive resident information may be shared only through authorized roles and permitted emergency processes.
- Detailed phone numbers, device instructions, reporting steps, and evacuation communication workflows live in the related response, resource, incident command, evacuation, and appendix documents.
