# Policy and Organizational Statements

## Purpose
This document explains who owns the emergency preparedness program, what the program is intended to do, and how {{facility_name}} keeps the written program aligned with outside response partners.

## Policy Statement
{{facility_name}} maintains a comprehensive emergency preparedness program that supports resident safety, staff coordination, and operational continuity during emergencies affecting the facility or surrounding community.

The program is written to work with local response systems, not apart from them. The facility documents its planning relationships with {{collaborative_partners}} and confirms alignment with {{local_response_agencies}} as part of its integrated response planning.

The emergency operations plan is the master operating document for disaster response. It identifies who can approve updates, who is responsible for annual review, and how significant revisions are communicated to staff. For this facility, the primary accountable role is {{plan_owner_title}}.

The written program should stay high-level in this topic. Detailed risk analysis, continuity procedures, and hazard-specific actions belong in their own documents so this policy statement remains easy to maintain and explain.

## What Staff Should Understand
- The emergency preparedness program applies to the whole facility, not only leadership.
- The written program is reviewed at least annually and whenever major changes are made.
- Outside coordination matters because the facility may need support, information sharing, or integrated action during a disaster.
- Detailed operational steps are maintained in linked topic documents rather than repeated here.
