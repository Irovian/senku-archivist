# Training and Testing Program

## Purpose
This document explains how {{facility_name}} maintains the emergency preparedness training and testing program and keeps it aligned with the emergency plan, risk assessment, policies and procedures, and communication plan.

## Program Framework
The training and testing program is part of the emergency preparedness program. It supports staff readiness, verifies that emergency procedures can be used in practice, and creates a structured way to identify improvement needs after training, drills, exercises, or actual events.

The program should be based on the facility's current emergency plan, hazard vulnerability analysis, emergency policies and procedures, communication plan, resident population needs, services provided, and continuity of operations expectations. Updates to those documents should be considered when the training and testing program is reviewed.

## Review and Ownership
The training and testing program is reviewed and updated at least annually. The role responsible for coordinating review and updates is {{training_testing_review_owner_title}}.

If state requirements, facility policy, or corporate policy require more frequent review, {{facility_name}} also reviews the training and testing program at least semiannually.

Review should consider training completion records, staff knowledge gaps, exercise results, actual event findings, after-action reports, regulatory changes, risk assessment updates, communication plan changes, and revisions to emergency procedures.

## Program Boundaries
This document owns the governance of the training and testing program. The Training document explains staff training expectations. The Annual Testing document explains exercise expectations, documentation, after-action review, and improvement tracking. Related incident command, all-hazards response, evacuation, recovery, and appendix documents contain role-specific tools and forms.

The program should be organized enough that surveyors, leadership, and staff can understand how training, testing, documentation, and improvement activities fit together without duplicating every training lesson or exercise scenario inside this governance topic.

## Coordination With Related Documents
This topic should be read with:

- Training, which describes initial and annual training expectations.
- Annual Testing, which describes exercise expectations and after-action improvement tracking.
- Risk Assessment Process, which informs training and testing priorities.
- Communications and emergency policies and procedures, which provide content that staff must understand and that exercises may test.
- Appendix and evidence documents, which maintain rosters, sign-in sheets, training materials, exercise documentation, and after-action records.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Training and testing are connected parts of the emergency preparedness program.
- The program is based on the current emergency plan, risk assessment, policies and procedures, and communication plan.
- Training records, exercise results, and actual events should feed improvement work.
- The program must be reviewed at least annually, and more often when required by state or facility policy.
- Detailed lesson materials, exercise tools, and after-action forms live in related documents and evidence files.
