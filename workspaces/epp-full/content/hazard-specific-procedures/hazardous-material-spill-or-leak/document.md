
# Hazardous Material Spill or Leak

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} protects residents, staff, visitors, and operations during a hazardous material spill, leak, chemical odor, or suspected exposure event.

## Immediate Actions
Staff should avoid contact with the material, isolate the area, warn others, and report the location, product if known, amount, odor, visible release, injuries, and exposure concerns. Staff should not attempt cleanup unless they are trained, equipped, and assigned to do so.

Residents, visitors, and staff should be moved away from the immediate hazard when safe. If fumes, vapors, or airborne exposure are suspected, leadership should consider closing doors, limiting traffic, adjusting air handling if appropriate, and contacting emergency responders.

## Identification and Notification
The safety data sheet or product label should be used when available to support response decisions. Incident command should notify maintenance, environmental services, clinical leadership, emergency responders, poison control, public health, regulatory agencies, vendors, or cleanup contractors as appropriate to the event.

## Resident Care and Exposure Management
Nursing should assess residents and staff for exposure symptoms and arrange medical evaluation when needed. Contaminated clothing, linens, or equipment should be handled according to infection prevention, hazardous material, and responder guidance.

## Cleanup and Recovery
Cleanup should occur only after the hazard is identified and appropriate controls are in place. Recovery includes area clearance, ventilation or environmental checks, replacement of supplies, waste handling, documentation of exposures and actions, and after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Stay away from unknown spills, leaks, vapors, or odors.
- Isolate the area and report details quickly.
- Do not clean a hazardous spill unless trained and directed.
- Move residents away from exposure when safe.
- Follow responder and leadership direction before reopening the area.
