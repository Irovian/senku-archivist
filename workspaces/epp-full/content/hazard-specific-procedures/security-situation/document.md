
# Security Situation

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} responds to security concerns that may threaten residents, staff, visitors, property, access control, or facility operations.

## Activation
A security situation may include violence, threats, unauthorized entry, suspicious behavior, theft, vandalism, disorderly conduct, civil disturbance near the facility, or any event requiring security assistance or law enforcement support.

## Immediate Actions
Staff should protect residents from immediate danger, move away from unsafe areas, request assistance, and report the situation to leadership. If violence, weapons, serious threats, or criminal activity are present, staff should call 911 or follow the emergency notification process.

Staff should avoid physical intervention unless required to protect life and no safer option exists. Staff should not investigate a suspected crime scene or disturb possible evidence unless necessary for safety or care.

## Leadership and Security Response
Leadership should assess operational impact, determine whether incident command or building lockdown is needed, and assign staff to control access, guide responders, support resident care, and monitor safe areas. Maintenance, security, or assigned staff may support door control, traffic direction, and scene protection until law enforcement arrives.

## Post-Incident Actions
After the immediate threat is controlled, leadership should account for residents, staff, and visitors; arrange medical or emotional support; preserve evidence as directed; document actions; and communicate through approved channels.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Report security concerns quickly.
- Keep residents away from unsafe areas.
- Do not investigate or disturb a possible crime scene.
- Follow law enforcement and leadership direction.
- Document what you saw and did as soon as practical.
