# Active Shooter / Person with a Weapon

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} protects residents, staff, visitors, and responders during an active shooter or person-with-a-weapon incident.

## Immediate Protective Actions
Any staff member who becomes aware of a weapon threat should protect life first, call 911 when safe, alert nearby staff using the safest available method, and follow law enforcement direction. Staff should use the practical response that best fits their location: evacuate if a safe exit is available, hide or secure in place if escape is unsafe, and take last-resort defensive action only when directly confronted.

Staff should not delay protective action while waiting for command-center instructions. Resident movement should be limited to actions that improve safety and can be performed without increasing exposure to danger.

## Command Center Coordination
The Incident Commander or assigned leader should coordinate with law enforcement, activate lockdown or evacuation procedures when directed or when safe, preserve communication with departments, and identify a safe location for leadership coordination if the normal command center cannot be used.

Leadership should be prepared to give responders building access information, known threat location, resident-care area information, hazardous-area concerns, utility or key information, and any known injured or missing persons.

## Resident, Staff, and Visitor Accountability
When conditions allow, departments should account for residents, staff, and visitors and report missing, injured, sheltered, evacuated, or separated persons to the command center. Family or responsible-party notification should be coordinated through assigned leadership and law enforcement guidance.

## Recovery
Recovery should begin only after law enforcement or the command center determines that the immediate threat is controlled. Recovery actions include medical response, reunification support, staff support, documentation, preservation of evidence when directed, family communication, and after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Protect life first; do not wait for formal activation when immediate danger exists.
- Call 911 when safe and provide the clearest location information possible.
- Evacuate, hide or secure in place, or take last-resort defensive action based on immediate conditions.
- Follow law enforcement direction when responders arrive.
- Account for residents, staff, and visitors only when it can be done safely.
