# Bomb Threat Call Log

## Purpose
This log supports staff who receive a bomb threat or similar telephone threat and preserves information needed by leadership and law enforcement.

## Use of Log
The staff member receiving the call should remain calm, keep the caller talking if possible, avoid interrupting, and record information while the call is in progress or immediately afterward. The staff member should alert a supervisor, Administrator, person in charge, or command center as soon as doing so is safe.

## Information to Capture
The log should capture the date and time of the call, exact words used by the caller, caller ID or number when available, where the device or threat is claimed to be, when it may occur, what it looks like, why the threat was made, caller voice characteristics, background sounds, language or accent, emotional state, and any other details that may help responders.

The log should also identify the staff member receiving the call, their department, call-back number or extension, who was notified, and the time notification occurred.

## Handling the Completed Log
The completed log should be given to the command center or law enforcement according to leadership direction. Staff should not share threat details broadly or speculate about the threat.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Keep the caller talking if possible and document exact words.
- Notify leadership as soon as it is safe.
- Record caller details, background sounds, claimed location, timing, and motive.
- Do not broadcast unverified threat details.
- Give the completed log to the command center or law enforcement.
