# Bomb Threat / Suspicious Package

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} responds to a bomb threat, suspicious package, or similar threat requiring search, notification, protection, and possible evacuation decisions.

## Receiving a Threat
A staff member receiving a threat by phone should remain calm, keep the caller on the line if possible, listen carefully, document exact words, note caller characteristics, and alert a supervisor or the person in charge as soon as it is safe to do so. Staff should use the Bomb Threat Call Log when available.

A staff member finding a suspicious package or object should not touch, move, open, cover, or use radios or phones immediately adjacent to the item unless directed by responders. The area should be cleared quietly and the command center notified.

## Command Center Actions
The command center should notify law enforcement, activate incident command as needed, determine whether a search is appropriate, assign search areas, preserve calm communication, and decide whether evacuation, partial evacuation, relocation, or sheltering is safest based on law enforcement guidance and facility conditions.

Searches should be performed by staff familiar with their areas when directed by leadership and when it is safe. Staff should look for items that are unusual for the area, report findings, and avoid handling suspicious objects.

## Resident Safety and Evacuation Decisions
Resident movement should be based on threat location, law enforcement guidance, life safety, weather, staffing, and resident acuity. If movement is needed, staff should follow evacuation, holding area, resident tracking, medication, and medical-record procedures.

## Recovery
The command center should restore operations only after clearance from law enforcement or assigned leadership, reconcile search documentation, document actions, communicate status updates, and capture improvement items for after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Keep the caller on the line and document as much information as possible.
- Do not touch or move a suspicious object.
- Search only if directed and only in a safe manner.
- Evacuation is a leadership and law-enforcement-guided decision.
- Use the Bomb Threat Call Log and Building Search Checklist when applicable.
