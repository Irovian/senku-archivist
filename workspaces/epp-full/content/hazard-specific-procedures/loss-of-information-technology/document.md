
# Loss of Information Technology

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} maintains resident care and operations during a loss of information technology, electronic records, network access, medication systems, communication systems, or other critical digital services.

## Immediate Actions
Staff should report the outage, affected systems, suspected cause if known, and resident-care impact. If advance warning exists, staff should save work, secure protected information, and follow instructions for safe shutdown or continuity steps.

Leadership and information technology support should assess the affected systems, expected duration, data access, cybersecurity concerns, backup availability, and need to activate downtime procedures.

## Downtime Operations
Departments should use approved paper backup, manual ordering, manual documentation, runner, phone, radio, fax, or other alternate processes as directed. Nursing, pharmacy, dietary, maintenance, business office, and other departments should maintain records needed to restore data when systems return.

Resident care priorities include medication administration, treatment documentation, orders, allergies, diet information, medical records, resident tracking, and communication with providers.

## Recovery
IT support should verify systems before staff resume normal use. Departments should reconcile paper records, re-enter required data, identify missing information, report suspected privacy or security incidents, and document downtime impacts.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Switch to downtime procedures when directed.
- Protect resident information during manual processes.
- Keep paper documentation clear, dated, and signed.
- Do not resume normal system use until cleared.
- Report privacy, medication, order, and record concerns quickly.
