
# Hurricane

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} prepares for and responds to a hurricane, tropical storm, or related wind, rain, flooding, access, staffing, and supply disruption.

## Preparedness and Monitoring
Leadership should monitor official weather and emergency management guidance during hurricane season or when a tropical system may affect the area. Departments should review emergency roles, staff contact information, resident needs, evacuation resources, transportation arrangements, supplies, fuel, communications, and building preparation needs.

When advance notice is available, leadership should determine whether to shelter in place, reduce census, relocate selected residents, or prepare for evacuation in coordination with local emergency officials.

## Watch or Threat Phase
Incident command should assess expected timing, wind, flooding, staffing, supply, utility, transportation, and evacuation risks. Departments should secure outdoor items, protect records and critical supplies, confirm medication and oxygen needs, update resident census, and prepare for restricted deliveries or delayed staff relief.

Residents and representatives should receive appropriate updates through the assigned communication process. Nonessential services and activities may be modified or cancelled when conditions require.

## Warning or Impact Phase
During dangerous conditions, staff should keep residents away from windows, maintain essential care, account for residents and staff, and report building damage, leaks, utility concerns, injuries, and resource shortages. Leadership should coordinate with emergency management and responders regarding evacuation orders, road access, and emergency support.

## Recovery
Recovery includes resident reassessment, damage and utility checks, supply restocking, vendor coordination, family or representative updates, cost tracking, documentation, and after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Follow official weather and leadership direction.
- Protect residents from wind, water, glass, and power-related risks.
- Report staffing, supply, utility, and building concerns quickly.
- Keep documentation current during extended operations.
