
# Tornado / High Winds

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} protects residents, staff, visitors, and operations during a tornado watch, tornado warning, or high-wind event.

## Watch Phase
When a tornado watch or high-wind threat is issued, leadership should monitor official weather information, prepare incident command as needed, and notify departments to prepare safe areas, emergency supplies, resident movement support, and building protections.

Departments should secure outdoor items when safe, move loose items away from windows, close blinds or curtains, check emergency lighting and communication methods, and prepare to relocate residents to interior areas if conditions worsen.

## Warning Phase
When a tornado warning is issued or immediate high-wind danger exists, staff should move residents away from windows and exterior walls to interior hallways or windowless rooms when safe. Residents who cannot be moved should be protected in place with available blankets, pillows, lowered beds, and distance from glass or flying debris.

Staff should reassure residents, avoid elevators unless cleared, and report injuries, damage, utility concerns, and resident movement status to the command center or assigned leader.

## Department Responsibilities
Maintenance should assess building, utility, generator, roof, exterior, and debris hazards when safe. Nursing should assess resident injuries, distress, medical equipment needs, and relocation needs. Leadership should coordinate with emergency management and determine whether evacuation, partial relocation, or continued sheltering is required.

## Recovery
Recovery includes injury assessment, structural and utility checks, debris control, resident and representative communication, restoration of services, cost tracking, and after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Move residents away from windows and exterior walls when safe.
- Protect residents in place if they cannot be moved.
- Wait until danger has passed before entering damaged areas.
- Report damage, injuries, and utility concerns quickly.
