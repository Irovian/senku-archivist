
# Pandemic Emergency Plan

## Purpose
This plan describes how {{facility_name}} maintains readiness for a pandemic or widespread infectious disease event requiring sustained infection prevention, staffing, supply, communication, and resident-care controls.

## Plan Maintenance
Leadership should review the pandemic emergency plan at least annually and after exercises, outbreaks, or real events. Review should include infection prevention policies, incident command roles, contact lists, vendor agreements, PPE inventory, staff training, fit testing where applicable, reporting processes, and communication methods.

## Activation
The plan may be activated when public health guidance, resident or staff illness trends, supply shortages, staffing disruption, or community transmission requires enhanced controls. Incident command should define operational periods, assign responsibilities, and coordinate with infection prevention, medical leadership, public health, vendors, and emergency management.

## Operations During a Pandemic
Departments should follow current infection prevention direction for screening, isolation, cohorting, PPE, environmental cleaning, visitation, communal activities, resident services, staffing, and supply conservation. Resident care should prioritize medications, treatments, nutrition, hydration, psychosocial needs, and communication with representatives.

## Communication and Recovery
Leadership should provide timely updates to residents, representatives, staff, providers, public health authorities, and other partners as appropriate. Recovery includes demobilizing temporary controls, replenishing supplies, supporting staff, updating training, documenting decisions, and completing after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Follow current pandemic and infection prevention direction.
- Report illness, exposure, staffing, and supply concerns quickly.
- Maintain resident dignity and communication during restrictions.
- Keep documentation current for decisions, notifications, and care changes.
