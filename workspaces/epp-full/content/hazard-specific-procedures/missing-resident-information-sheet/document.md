
# Missing Resident Information Sheet

## Purpose
This sheet identifies the information {{facility_name}} should gather and share during a missing resident response.

## Resident Identification
Record the resident's name, date, unit or room, age, sex, height, weight, hair color or style, other identifying features, and a current photograph if available.

## Last Known Information
Record the time and place the resident was last seen, who last saw the resident, clothing or footwear last observed, assistive devices used, communication ability, ambulatory status, mental status, and immediate health risks.

## Search and Risk Information
Record likely destinations, prior elopement history, prior locations where the resident was found, smoking status if relevant, weather or exposure concerns, possible transportation access, and whether another person may be involved.

## Contacts and Notifications
Record the resident representative or next of kin, phone number, physician or provider contacts if needed, police notification time, regulatory notification if required, and staff member completing the form.

## Use During the Response
The assigned leader should provide relevant information from this sheet to search teams, law enforcement, leadership, nursing, and communication staff. The completed sheet should be retained with the incident documentation and referenced during care-plan review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Gather facts quickly and clearly.
- Attach or provide a current photo when available.
- Share only the information needed for the search and required notifications.
- Update the sheet if new facts are learned.
