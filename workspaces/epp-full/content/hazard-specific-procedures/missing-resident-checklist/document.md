
# Missing Resident Checklist

## Purpose
This checklist helps {{facility_name}} assign, track, and document search areas during a missing resident response.

## Resident and Event Information
Record the resident name, unit or room, date and time the resident was determined missing, time last seen, person last seen by, and the leader coordinating the search.

## Search Assignment Tracking
For each assigned area, record the area searched, staff member assigned, time search started, time search completed, and findings. Completed searches should be reported to the command center or assigned leader before staff move to a new area.

## Interior Search Areas
Search assignments should include resident room and bathroom, nearby rooms, empty beds, closets, shower rooms, lounges, dining areas, activity spaces, therapy areas, elevators, stairwells, offices, storage rooms, mechanical rooms that are accessible, and other locations the resident may reasonably access.

## Exterior Search Areas
Exterior assignments should include exits, courtyards, parking areas, sidewalks, loading areas, entrances, public transit or pickup locations, shrubs or concealed areas, nearby paths, and other likely destinations based on resident history.

## Follow-Up Documentation
Record areas that could not be searched, reasons they were not searched, law enforcement notification if made, representative notification, resident found time, resident condition, and any immediate corrective actions.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Search thoroughly, including hidden or unlikely locations.
- Report each completed area promptly.
- Do not assume another team searched an area unless it is documented.
- Update the assigned leader if new risk information is learned.
