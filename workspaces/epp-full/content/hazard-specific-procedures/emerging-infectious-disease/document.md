
# Emerging Infectious Disease

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} prepares for and responds to an emerging infectious disease event that may affect residents, staff, visitors, vendors, and continuity of operations.

## Activation
Leadership should monitor public health guidance, local conditions, resident illness trends, staffing availability, and supply status. The emergency plan may be activated when an infectious disease threat requires enhanced infection prevention controls, staffing adjustments, restricted access, resident cohorting, alternate communication methods, or coordination with public health authorities.

## Immediate Actions
Incident command should define the operational period, assign responsibility for infection prevention coordination, and confirm reporting expectations. Departments should reinforce hand hygiene, respiratory hygiene, environmental cleaning, symptom monitoring, and appropriate use of personal protective equipment.

Staff should promptly report resident or staff symptoms, exposure concerns, PPE shortages, testing needs, and barriers to resident care. Visitor, vendor, and communal activity practices should follow current leadership direction and applicable public health guidance.

## Resident Care and Services
Nursing and clinical leadership should evaluate resident risk, determine isolation or cohorting needs, maintain essential treatments and medications, and monitor changes in condition. Dietary, environmental services, activities, social services, and therapy should adapt services to reduce transmission risk while preserving resident safety and dignity.

## Staffing and Supplies
Leadership should monitor staffing levels, call-in needs, staff illness, and critical supplies. Resource requests should be escalated through incident command when supplies, equipment, testing materials, or staffing cannot support ongoing operations.

## Communication and Recovery
The facility should communicate with residents, representatives, staff, medical providers, public health authorities, vendors, and emergency management as appropriate. Recovery includes demobilizing temporary controls, replenishing supplies, supporting staff, documenting decisions, and completing after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Follow current infection prevention direction for the event.
- Report symptoms, exposures, supply concerns, and resident changes quickly.
- Do not change isolation, cohorting, or access practices without leadership direction.
- Maintain resident dignity, hydration, nutrition, medications, and communication with representatives.
- Document actions taken during the response.
