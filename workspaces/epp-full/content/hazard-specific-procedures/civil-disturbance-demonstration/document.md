# Civil Disturbance / Demonstration

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} protects residents, staff, visitors, and property during a civil disturbance, demonstration, unauthorized assembly, or crowd-related security concern.

## Activation
Staff should notify the person in charge, command center, and security or assigned support immediately when a disturbance or demonstration is observed on or near facility property. Leadership should contact law enforcement when safety, access, intimidation, property damage, or disruption of care may occur.

## Facility Actions
The command center should evaluate whether to restrict access, lock or monitor entry points, reroute staff and visitors, protect sensitive areas, secure utilities and deliveries, and limit resident movement near affected areas.

Staff should avoid arguing with participants, attempting physical removal, or escalating the situation. All interactions should prioritize safety, calm communication, and law enforcement coordination.

## Resident Care and Communication
Departments should continue essential resident care, keep residents away from affected areas, and report fear, distress, blocked access, or safety concerns. Staff should use approved communication channels for updates and should not share unverified information.

## Recovery
After the disturbance resolves, leadership should confirm that unauthorized persons have left, restore access control, assess damage or service disruption, document actions, communicate status updates, and capture after-action items.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Notify leadership quickly when an unauthorized assembly or disturbance is observed.
- Do not confront or physically engage participants unless immediate life safety requires action.
- Follow access-control and lockdown instructions.
- Keep residents away from affected areas.
- Use approved communication channels and avoid speculation.
