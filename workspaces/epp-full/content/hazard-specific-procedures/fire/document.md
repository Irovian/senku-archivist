
# Fire

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} protects residents, staff, visitors, and operations during a fire, smoke condition, or suspected fire emergency.

## Immediate Response
Staff who discover smoke or fire should protect anyone in immediate danger, activate the fire alarm or emergency notification process, and report the location and nature of the event. Staff should close doors to help confine smoke and fire when it can be done safely.

Staff may use a fire extinguisher only when trained, the fire is small, the exit path is clear, and resident safety is not delayed. Life safety, resident movement, and alarm activation take priority over property protection.

## Resident Protection
Residents in immediate danger should be moved beyond smoke, heat, or fire exposure. If full evacuation is not required, staff should follow horizontal relocation or shelter-in-place direction from leadership and responding fire officials.

Nursing should account for residents, prioritize residents who require oxygen, mobility assistance, or close monitoring, and communicate clinical concerns to incident command.

## Department Responsibilities
Incident command or the most senior leader on site should coordinate with the fire department, determine whether evacuation or relocation is needed, and maintain communication with departments. Maintenance or assigned staff should support building-system checks, utility controls, fire protection system status, and access for responders.

Departments should preserve clear corridors, avoid elevator use unless approved by responders, and report missing residents, injuries, smoke migration, utility concerns, and damaged equipment.

## Recovery
Operations should not return to affected areas until cleared by leadership and appropriate authorities. Recovery includes resident reassessment, room or area cleanup, fire system reset or repair, documentation, family or representative communication, and after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Protect residents and activate the alarm quickly.
- Close doors when safe to reduce smoke and fire spread.
- Do not use elevators during a fire response unless specifically cleared.
- Use extinguishers only if trained and safe.
- Follow leadership and fire department direction for relocation or evacuation.
