# Carbon Monoxide Alarm Activation

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} responds to a carbon monoxide alarm, suspected carbon monoxide exposure, or unsafe combustion source.

## Activation
Any staff member who hears a carbon monoxide alarm, observes symptoms that may indicate exposure, or identifies a suspected source should notify a supervisor, Maintenance, the command center, or the person in charge immediately. If the facility uses a general emergency alert, staff should follow {{emergency_code_alert}} instructions when announced.

## Immediate Actions
Staff should move residents, staff, and visitors away from the affected area when safe, call 911 or emergency responders as directed, and avoid entering a suspected contaminated area without responder guidance. Doors and windows may be opened only when doing so is safe and does not conflict with fire, security, or responder direction.

Maintenance or assigned staff should identify possible fuel-burning equipment or other sources, shut down suspected sources when safe, and coordinate with emergency responders, utility providers, or service vendors.

## Resident Care and Operations
Nursing should assess residents and staff for symptoms, prioritize residents with respiratory or cardiac vulnerability, and coordinate medical evaluation when needed. The command center should consider relocation, partial evacuation, ventilation limits, and continuity of essential services.

## Recovery
The affected area should not return to normal use until cleared by appropriate responders, Maintenance, or assigned leadership. Recovery includes documenting actions, equipment status, resident or staff symptoms, repairs, communication, and after-action needs.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Treat carbon monoxide alarms and suspected exposure as urgent safety events.
- Move away from the affected area when directed and safe.
- Do not re-enter a suspected contaminated area until cleared.
- Report resident or staff symptoms immediately.
- Maintenance and responders coordinate source control and clearance.
