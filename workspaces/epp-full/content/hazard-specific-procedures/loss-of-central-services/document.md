
# Loss of Central Services

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} coordinates response when one or more central services are disrupted and resident care or facility operations may be affected.

## Scope
Central services may include utilities, communications, information systems, food service, laundry, medical gases, HVAC, water, sewer, transportation, vendor services, or other shared systems that support multiple departments. This procedure is an umbrella response and should be used with the applicable service-specific procedure.

## Initial Assessment
Leadership should identify the disrupted service, affected areas, expected duration, resident-care impact, staffing impact, safety risks, and available workarounds. Departments should report essential-service gaps and complete rapid assessments when requested.

Incident command should be activated when the disruption affects resident safety, regulatory reporting, staffing, evacuation decisions, public communication, or multiple departments.

## Operational Response
Departments should implement downtime, manual, alternate vendor, relocation, conservation, or service-specific procedures as appropriate. Leadership should prioritize services needed for life safety, medications, treatments, hydration, nutrition, sanitation, temperature control, communication, and resident tracking.

## Communication and Recovery
Leadership should coordinate with vendors, emergency management, regulators, residents, representatives, and staff as appropriate. Recovery includes restoring service, validating system function, replenishing supplies, documenting costs and actions, and completing after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Report service disruptions and resident-care effects quickly.
- Use the service-specific procedure for detailed actions.
- Conserve resources when directed.
- Document downtime actions, workarounds, and unresolved needs.
