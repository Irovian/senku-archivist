
# Snow Emergency / Ice Storm

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} protects residents, staff, visitors, and operations during a snow emergency, ice storm, extreme winter access disruption, or related utility risk.

## Activation
Leadership should activate the response when weather conditions may limit staffing, deliveries, emergency responder access, transportation, fire department response, safe exits, or essential services. Departments should prepare for modified staffing and extended operations if travel becomes unsafe.

## Immediate Operational Priorities
Incident command should monitor official weather guidance, assess resident care impact, confirm staffing plans, and determine whether nonessential services should be modified or postponed. Staff may be asked to remain on duty until safe relief is available.

Maintenance or assigned staff should keep exits, sidewalks, hydrants, fire department connections, access roads, and key entrances as clear and safe as possible. Roof, tree, branch, generator, heating, and utility concerns should be monitored as conditions allow.

## Resident Care and Supplies
Nursing should confirm medications, treatments, high-risk residents, oxygen, medical equipment, and staffing needs. Food service, housekeeping, laundry, and supply areas should assess available stock and adjust menus, linen plans, and staff support when deliveries or shift changes are delayed.

## Fire and Life Safety
If responder access may be delayed or fire protection concerns arise, leadership should consider additional rounds or fire watch practices consistent with related procedures.

## Recovery
Recovery includes clearing access, reassessing residents, restoring normal schedules, restocking supplies, documenting costs and decisions, and reviewing response performance.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Stay on duty until released when travel conditions affect relief.
- Keep exits and emergency access clear when assigned.
- Report heating, power, access, supply, and resident-care concerns quickly.
- Follow staffing, sheltering, and modified service direction.
