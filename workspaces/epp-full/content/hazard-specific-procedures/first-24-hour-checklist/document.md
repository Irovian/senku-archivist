
# First 24-Hour Checklist

## Purpose
This checklist supports the first operational period after {{facility_name}} activates emergency operations for a hazard, utility failure, evacuation threat, staffing disruption, or other significant incident.

## First Operational Priorities
- Activate incident command or identify the leader in charge.
- Confirm resident, staff, visitor, and responder life-safety needs.
- Identify the incident type, affected areas, immediate hazards, and expected duration.
- Assign documentation responsibility for decisions, actions, costs, and communications.
- Establish internal communication methods and reporting intervals.

## Resident Care
- Account for residents and identify missing, injured, relocated, or high-risk residents.
- Prioritize residents dependent on oxygen, dialysis, power, specialized equipment, or close monitoring.
- Confirm medications, treatments, hydration, nutrition, toileting, and infection prevention needs.
- Track resident movement, transfers, discharges, and representative notifications.

## Staffing and Operations
- Confirm staffing levels by department and shift.
- Identify call-in needs, transportation barriers, staff sheltering needs, and fatigue risks.
- Assign staff to essential functions, resident movement, communications, supply tracking, and documentation.
- Determine whether routine services should continue, pause, or be modified.

## Supplies, Systems, and Communications
- Assess power, water, sewer, HVAC, communications, fire protection, medical gases, food service, and building access.
- Check critical supplies, PPE, food, water, fuel, linens, medications, and transport equipment.
- Contact emergency management, public health, vendors, receiving facilities, or other partners as needed.
- Prepare resident, representative, staff, and provider updates.

## Planning for the Next Period
- Identify unresolved hazards, resource gaps, staffing risks, resident care risks, and regulatory notifications.
- Decide whether to shelter in place, relocate internally, reduce census, evacuate, or request outside assistance.
- Set the next briefing time and update the action plan.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Life safety, resident care, documentation, and communication come first.
- Report changes in resident status, staffing, systems, and supplies quickly.
- Use the command structure for decisions and resource requests.
