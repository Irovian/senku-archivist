# Loss of Steam Pressure

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} responds when steam pressure is reduced, unstable, unavailable, or unable to support steam-dependent services.

## Recognition and Activation
Steam pressure loss may affect heating, domestic hot water, laundry, kitchen equipment, sterilization support, or other facility-specific services. Staff should report loss of steam pressure, equipment alarms, temperature concerns, service disruption, or department impacts immediately.

The command center or assigned leader should determine whether the event requires alternate heat, linen conservation, menu changes, service reduction, vendor support, regulatory notification, relocation, evacuation, or activation of related heating, electric, gas, water, or cooking procedures.

Notification to {{regulatory_agency_name}} or another authority should occur when required by the event, duration, resident impact, temperature impact, sanitation impact, or facility policy.

## Steam System Assessment
{{maintenance_resource_owner_title}} should assess the steam source, expected outage duration, pressure loss, affected equipment, boiler interface, power or fuel dependency, hot water impact, heating impact, laundry impact, and any unsafe equipment conditions.

The steam system is described as {{steam_system_description}}. Steam-dependent services include {{steam_dependent_services}}. Alternate heat sources, vendors, or warmed areas include {{alternate_heat_sources}}.

## Department Impacts
Nursing should identify residents affected by loss of heat, hot water, bathing, infection prevention support, sterilization support, or other steam-dependent services. Dining services should adjust menus or preparation methods when steam-supported kitchen equipment is unavailable.

{{environmental_resource_owner_title}} should coordinate linen conservation, housekeeping changes, and sanitation impacts. Alternate laundry service or linen support includes {{alternate_laundry_service}}.

{{dining_resource_owner_title}} should coordinate food-service changes when steam loss affects cooking, warming, dishwashing, or safe meal preparation.

## Operating Under Steam Loss
The command center or assigned leader should prioritize resident safety, safe temperatures, sanitation, hygiene, meal service, and clinical support. Departments should conserve linen, modify bathing or showering when needed, use approved alternate cleaning methods, and report service impacts.

If steam loss is caused by electric service loss, generator failure, gas/propane service loss, heating system failure, water loss, or loss of cooking ability, the related procedure should also be activated.

## Recovery
Before normal steam-dependent services resume, maintenance should confirm that steam pressure is stable, affected systems are safe, boilers or related equipment are operating properly, and departments understand which services may restart.

Departments should document service impacts, linen conservation, meal-service changes, resident-care impacts, vendor actions, repairs, emergency purchases, and lessons learned.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Report steam pressure problems immediately.
- Steam loss can affect heat, hot water, laundry, dining, sanitation, and resident care.
- Linen, bathing, and meal-service changes should be coordinated through the command center or assigned leader.
- Electric, gas, and heating failures may cause or worsen steam loss.
- Telephone/internal communications issues remain in their own procedure.
