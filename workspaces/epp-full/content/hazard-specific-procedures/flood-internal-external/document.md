
# Flood (Internal / External)

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} responds to internal flooding, external flooding, water intrusion, or flood-related threats to resident care and facility operations.

## Activation
Leadership should activate the emergency response process when water threatens resident areas, utilities, medical equipment, medication storage, food service, records, access routes, or safe occupancy. The response should be scaled to the source, rate of spread, forecast, and operational impact.

## Immediate Actions
Staff should protect residents from immediate hazards, keep people away from standing water when electrical risk may exist, and report the location, source, and spread of water. Maintenance or assigned staff should evaluate shutoff needs, drainage, sump or pump function, roof or window leaks, sewer backup, and utility risks.

Departments should move residents, equipment, supplies, medications, food, and records away from affected areas when it is safe to do so. Wet floors, contaminated water, and blocked exits should be reported promptly.

## Resident Care and Operations
Nursing should reassess affected residents, prioritize residents who depend on power or medical equipment, and coordinate relocation within the facility if rooms or care areas are unsafe. Leadership should determine whether shelter-in-place, partial relocation, census reduction, or evacuation is needed.

## External Flood Considerations
When external flooding affects roads, parking, vendors, staffing, or emergency response access, incident command should monitor local emergency management direction, communicate access limitations, and preserve critical supplies.

## Recovery
Recovery includes water removal, environmental cleaning, infection-control review, utility and building-system checks, supply replacement, damage documentation, cost tracking, and after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Keep residents away from water, electrical hazards, and contaminated areas.
- Report the source and spread of water quickly.
- Protect medications, records, supplies, and equipment when safe.
- Follow leadership direction for relocation or evacuation.
- Do not reoccupy affected areas until cleared.
