# Loss of Natural Gas / Propane

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} responds when natural gas, propane, or another gas fuel supply is interrupted, unavailable, restricted, or shut off for safety reasons.

## Recognition and Activation
Gas or propane loss may affect heating, cooking, hot water, laundry, boilers, dryers, kitchen equipment, and other fuel-dependent services. Staff should report suspected service loss, appliance shutdowns, pilot-light failure, utility notices, fuel delivery problems, or department service impacts immediately.

This procedure addresses loss of gas or propane service. If staff smell gas, suspect a leak, or identify an immediate hazardous condition, the Natural Gas / Propane Odor / Leak procedure should be activated first.

The command center or assigned leader should determine whether the event requires service modification, resident relocation, alternate meal service, alternate heat, vendor support, regulatory notification, evacuation, or activation of related heating, cooking, electric, or water procedures.

Notification to {{regulatory_agency_name}} or another authority should occur when required by the event, duration, resident impact, life-safety impact, or facility policy.

## Gas Service Assessment
{{maintenance_resource_owner_title}} should assess the expected duration, affected areas, affected equipment, utility or supplier status, shut-off valves, appliance safety, pilot lights, boilers, hot water systems, laundry equipment, kitchen equipment, and other dependent services.

The facility gas service type is {{gas_service_type}}. Gas-dependent equipment, appliances, or services include {{gas_dependent_equipment}}. The gas utility, propane supplier, or emergency service contact is {{gas_utility_contact}}.

{{boiler_fuel_responsibility_description}} should be reviewed when the gas or propane loss affects boiler, hot water, heat, or other fuel-dependent building operations.

## Operational Priorities
The command center or assigned leader should prioritize resident safety, safe indoor temperatures, food service continuity, hot water needs, infection prevention, laundry and linen support, and safe equipment shutdown or restart.

{{dining_resource_owner_title}} should coordinate meal-service changes, food-safety decisions, and alternate cooking or meal support. Alternate cooking or meal preparation methods include {{alternate_cooking_methods}}.

{{environmental_resource_owner_title}} should coordinate laundry, linen conservation, housekeeping changes, and any sanitation impacts caused by loss of hot water, dryers, or other gas-dependent services.

## Department Coordination
Nursing and clinical leaders should identify residents whose care may be affected by loss of heat, hot water, laundry, sanitation, or meal-service changes. Residents who cannot be safely supported under service limitations should be considered for relocation, transfer, or alternate arrangements.

Maintenance should follow facility-approved procedures and utility or vendor direction before restoring gas service, relighting pilot lights, restarting appliances, or placing equipment back into service. Staff should not operate gas valves, pilot lights, or gas appliances unless trained and authorized to do so.

## Recovery
Before normal gas-dependent service resumes, responsible staff should confirm that the source of interruption has been addressed, affected appliances and equipment have been checked, pilot lights or ignition systems are safely restored where applicable, and departments understand when normal operations may restart.

Remaining service impacts, vendor actions, repairs, supply use, resident-care impacts, and lessons learned should be included in recovery documentation.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Report gas or propane service loss immediately.
- A gas odor or suspected leak is a life-safety event and uses the odor/leak procedure.
- Gas or propane loss can affect heat, hot water, cooking, laundry, and resident care.
- Only trained and authorized personnel should operate gas valves, pilot lights, or appliances.
- Detailed heating and cooking procedures remain in their dedicated documents.
