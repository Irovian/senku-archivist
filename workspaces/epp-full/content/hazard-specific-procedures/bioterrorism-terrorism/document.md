# Bioterrorism / Terrorism

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} responds to terrorism threats, suspected bioterrorism, suspicious contamination, or security conditions that may affect facility safety.

## Activation
The command center or assigned leader should activate this procedure when credible threat information is received, public safety agencies issue terrorism guidance affecting the facility, suspicious substances or packages are identified, residents or staff show concerning exposure patterns, or authorities request heightened security or monitoring.

## Immediate Actions
Staff should report suspicious activity, unusual packages, threats, unexplained illness clusters, contaminated mail, or security concerns immediately. Staff should not touch, move, open, smell, taste, or attempt to clean suspicious substances unless directed by trained responders.

The command center should notify 911 or appropriate authorities, secure affected areas, limit movement, consider lockdown or access control, preserve potential evidence, and coordinate with public health, emergency management, law enforcement, or hazardous materials responders as appropriate.

## Department Coordination
Departments should continue essential resident care while following safety restrictions. Nursing should monitor residents for signs of exposure or illness. Maintenance, environmental services, receiving, food service, laundry, and security-related staff should follow command-center direction for area control, shutdowns, sanitation limits, and supply protection.

## Communication and Recovery
Threat information should be communicated through approved channels and should avoid speculation. Public messaging should be coordinated by assigned leadership. Recovery may include responder clearance, environmental cleaning or decontamination, resident monitoring, staff support, documentation, and after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Report suspicious threats, substances, packages, and illness patterns immediately.
- Do not handle suspicious materials unless directed by trained responders.
- Follow access control, lockdown, isolation, or movement restrictions.
- Continue essential resident care within safety limits.
- Use approved communication channels and avoid spreading unverified information.
