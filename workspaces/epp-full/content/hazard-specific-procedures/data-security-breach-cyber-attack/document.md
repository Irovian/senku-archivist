# Data Security Breach - Cyber Attack

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} responds to a suspected data security breach, cyber attack, ransomware event, electronic health record outage, or compromise of information systems.

## Activation
Staff should report suspected cyber incidents immediately, including unusual system behavior, locked or encrypted files, suspicious messages, unauthorized access, missing devices, privacy concerns, or inability to access resident information.

## Immediate Actions
Staff should not attempt informal fixes, forward suspicious messages, connect unknown devices, or continue using systems that leadership or IT has restricted. The command center or assigned leader should coordinate with IT support, privacy or compliance leadership, clinical leadership, and vendors as needed.

If clinical systems are unavailable, departments should activate downtime procedures for resident care, medication administration, documentation, orders, communication, and information sharing.

## Resident Care and Privacy
Resident care continuity remains the priority. Staff should use approved downtime records, protect confidential information, verify orders and medication information through approved backup processes, and limit access to information to workforce members who need it for care or response.

## Communication and Recovery
Communication about the incident should be coordinated through assigned leadership. Recovery may include system isolation, password or access changes, restoration from backup, privacy review, required notifications, reconciliation of downtime documentation, and after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Report suspected cyber or privacy incidents immediately.
- Stop using systems if IT or leadership directs it.
- Use downtime procedures to maintain resident care.
- Protect resident information during manual workarounds.
- Do not speculate about the incident or share unapproved details.
