
# Full Recovery Structure

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} confirms that facility operations, systems, departments, and services have returned to normal or to an approved stable operating status after an emergency.

## Recovery Domains
Full recovery review should include structure, utilities, food services, resident services, information technology, staffing, communications, life safety systems, supplies, vendor support, and documentation.

## Verification
Leadership should confirm that appropriate internal leaders, contractors, utilities, public agencies, regulators, or other qualified parties have inspected or cleared affected areas and systems when required. Departments should confirm that required repairs, cleaning, restocking, documentation, and resident-care follow-up are complete.

## Department Recovery
Utilities should confirm power, fuel, water, sewer, HVAC, medical gases, generators, and related systems. Food services should confirm safe food storage, preparation, inventory, sanitation, and staffing. Resident services should confirm medications, clinical supplies, resident areas, documentation, and care routines. Information technology should confirm systems, data, devices, and downtime reconciliation. Staffing should confirm safe staffing and support service capacity.

## Closure
Incident command should not close recovery until unresolved risks are assigned, documented, and tracked. Final recovery should include cost documentation, communication follow-up, after-action review, and plan updates.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Full recovery requires more than reopening the building.
- Each department must confirm readiness and unresolved issues.
- Keep recovery documentation organized for review.
- Continue workarounds until leadership clears normal operations.
