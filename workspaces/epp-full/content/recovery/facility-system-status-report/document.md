
# Facility System Status Report

## Purpose
This report helps {{facility_name}} document the operational status of major facility systems during recovery.

## Report Timing
Leadership should request a facility system status report after an incident that may affect building function, utilities, communications, resident-care systems, life safety systems, access, security, or support services.

## Systems to Assess
The report should identify whether each system is fully functional, partially functional, or nonfunctional. Systems may include internal and external communications, nurse call, information technology, fire alarm and suppression, roadways and access, food preparation, laundry, structural components, isolation rooms, security systems, electrical power, generator, water, sewer, natural gas or propane, HVAC, elevators, medical gases, vacuum, steam, sump pumps, command center, and staff housing areas.

## Required Detail
For any system not fully functional, the report should include location, reason, impact, who inspected or reported the issue, immediate workaround, resources needed, responsible party, and estimated time to repair or restore.

## Use in Recovery
Incident command should use the report to prioritize repairs, determine resident-care risk, support regulatory notifications, coordinate vendors, document costs, and decide whether areas can reopen.

## What Staff Should Understand
- System status must be factual and current.
- Partial function still needs explanation and workaround planning.
- Do not assume a system is safe for use until cleared.
- Update the report as repairs or conditions change.
