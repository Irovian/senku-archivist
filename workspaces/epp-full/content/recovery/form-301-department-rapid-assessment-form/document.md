
# Form 301 - Department Rapid Assessment Form

## Purpose
Form 301 helps {{facility_name}} collect rapid department status information during response and recovery.

## When to Complete
The person in charge of each unit or department should complete the form when directed by incident command or when department status may affect resident care, evacuation, staffing, supplies, utilities, or operational capability.

## Information to Capture
The form should capture date, time, unit or department, location, person in charge, staffing by role, minimum safe staffing status, recall needs, current resident census, residents who may discharge home, evacuation transportation needs, critical equipment and supply status, and technology or utility status.

## Operational Capability
Departments should state whether they are fully operational, operating with limited capability, or non-operational. The form should describe barriers, staff relief needs, cleanup needs, supply limits, equipment problems, utility issues, and resident-care risks.

## Forward Planning
Departments should estimate how long they can operate at current staffing and supply levels and identify services that must be restored first to regain full function.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Complete the form quickly and accurately when assigned.
- Include resource gaps and resident-care impact.
- Update the command center if status changes after submission.
- Keep copies with incident and recovery documentation.
