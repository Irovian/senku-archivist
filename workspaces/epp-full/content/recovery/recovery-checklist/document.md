
# Recovery Checklist

## Purpose
This checklist supports {{facility_name}} in confirming that the environment of care is safe enough to continue, restore, or expand operations after a disaster.

## Core Review Areas
Recovery review should address access and egress, ambulance access, building safety, fire protection, internal and external communications, food service, electrical systems, emergency power, resident-care equipment, oxygen or medical gas systems if applicable, maintenance systems, HVAC, water, sewer, sanitation, infection prevention, staffing, supplies, security, and documentation.

## Clearance Before Use
Areas, systems, and equipment should not return to normal use until leadership determines that appropriate inspections, repairs, environmental checks, and regulatory or authority clearances have been completed when required.

## Department Input
Department leaders should confirm whether they have adequate staff, equipment, supplies, communication, resident-care capacity, and safe workspace to resume assigned services. Any limitation should be reported with the workaround and resources needed.

## Documentation
The checklist should record yes/no status, initials or responsible person, comments, unresolved items, repair needs, and follow-up assignments. Completed checklist items should be retained with recovery documentation.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Recovery clearance is not the same as emergency stabilization.
- Report what is not operational, not safe, or not staffed.
- Use approved workarounds until normal systems are restored.
- Keep checklist documentation current for review and follow-up.
