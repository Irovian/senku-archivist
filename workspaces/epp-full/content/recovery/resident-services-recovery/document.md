
# Resident Services Recovery

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} restores resident services after an emergency or disaster while maintaining resident safety, dignity, and communication.

## Recovery Priorities
Resident services recovery should address nursing care, medications, treatments, physician or provider communication, medical records, resident tracking, representative communication, psychosocial support, activities, therapy, social services, personal belongings, infection prevention, and room or unit readiness.

## Assessment
Clinical and resident-service leaders should assess resident condition, unmet care needs, delayed treatments, transfers or relocations, high-risk residents, staffing, supplies, equipment, documentation gaps, and family or representative communication needs. Any resident moved during the incident should be tracked and reassessed.

## Restoring Services
Leadership should prioritize services needed to stabilize resident care and return residents to normal routines. Recovery may require modified schedules, catch-up documentation, provider orders, pharmacy coordination, therapy or activity adjustments, belongings reconciliation, and additional resident or family updates.

## Documentation and Follow-Up
Documentation should include resident assessments, notifications, transfers, care-plan updates, incidents, delayed services, representative communication, and unresolved issues requiring follow-up.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Resident safety and dignity guide recovery decisions.
- Reassess residents after relocation, disruption, or service delay.
- Keep representatives informed through assigned channels.
- Document care gaps, follow-up needs, and recovery actions.
