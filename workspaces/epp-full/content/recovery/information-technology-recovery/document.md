
# Information Technology Recovery

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} restores information technology systems and reconciles downtime operations after an emergency or disaster.

## Recovery Priorities
Information technology recovery should prioritize systems that support resident care, medication administration, medical records, orders, pharmacy interfaces, communications, security, building automation, payroll or staffing, business operations, and protected information.

## System Verification
Information technology support should verify that affected systems, networks, devices, interfaces, backups, user access, and data integrity are functioning before departments resume normal use. If a privacy or security incident is suspected, leadership should follow privacy and security reporting procedures.

## Downtime Reconciliation
Departments should maintain paper documentation until system use is cleared. Once systems are restored, departments should reconcile downtime records, enter required data, verify orders and medications, identify missing information, and retain downtime documentation according to policy.

## Documentation and Follow-Up
Recovery documentation should include affected systems, outage duration, restoration steps, vendor support, data concerns, departments cleared for use, unresolved risks, costs, and after-action findings.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Do not resume normal system use until cleared.
- Protect resident information during recovery and reconciliation.
- Keep downtime records organized and legible.
- Report missing data, access problems, and privacy concerns quickly.
