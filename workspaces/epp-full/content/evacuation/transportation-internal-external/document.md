# Transportation (Internal / External)

## Purpose
This document describes internal and external transportation coordination during evacuation at {{facility_name}}.

## Internal Transportation
Internal transportation includes moving residents from rooms, units, apartments, care areas, or departments to holding areas, exits, discharge points, or transportation pickup locations. The Logistics Section Chief, Operations Section Chief, Labor Pool Unit Leader, or designee should coordinate wheelchairs, walkers, stretchers, evacuation devices, oxygen carts, and other movement equipment.

When elevators are cleared for use, staff should follow the route plan for {{elevator_locations}}. When elevators cannot be used, staff should follow safe stairwell or alternate movement plans, including {{alternate_vertical_transport_methods}} when applicable.

Reusable equipment should be cleaned or turned around as needed before it is sent back for additional resident movement.

## External Transportation
External transportation includes ambulances, wheelchair vans, buses, facility vehicles, partner vehicles, and other approved transport resources. Command should coordinate external transportation needs through emergency services, receiving partners, vendors, mutual aid, or other transportation sources.

Transportation decisions should reflect resident acuity, mobility, infection-control needs, oxygen or equipment needs, supervision needs, distance to destination, and available staff to accompany residents. Receiving options should be coordinated through {{receiving_facility_partners}} and the separate receiving-facility resources.

## Staging and Traffic Flow
The Safety/Security/Liaison Officer or designee should establish vehicle staging, traffic flow, access control, and pickup locations. Staff should keep evacuation paths, entrances, exits, and vehicle approaches clear.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Internal movement and external transport are coordinated separately but must stay aligned.
- Resident needs determine the type of transportation required.
- Equipment must be staged, reused safely, and tracked.
- Vehicle staging and traffic flow should be controlled by assigned staff or emergency responders.
