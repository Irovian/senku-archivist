# Assignment of Resident Evacuation Staff

## Purpose
This document explains how {{facility_name}} assigns staff to support resident movement during evacuation.

## Assignment Process
The Planning Section Chief, Labor Pool Unit Leader, Operations Section Chief, or designee should assign resident evacuation staff based on the evacuation order, resident needs, route conditions, available equipment, and staff qualifications.

Assignments may include floor evacuation teams, elevator evacuation teams, stairwell evacuation teams, discharge or exit teams, holding area staff, equipment runners, documentation support, and staff who accompany residents during transport when needed.

## Resident-Care Coordination
Resident-care leaders should identify residents who need additional assistance, specialized equipment, oxygen, behavior support, memory-care precautions, isolation precautions, or clinical monitoring. Clinical staff should remain responsible for resident-care decisions and should not be replaced by non-clinical evacuation support.

The staff member leading the affected unit or area should confirm when evacuation groups are present, explain the resident destination or holding area, and coordinate handoff before residents leave the area. Staff should communicate any missing records, medications, belongings, equipment, or family-notification concerns to command.

## Accountability
Assigned staff should report status to the labor pool, command center, or receiving leader as directed. Staff should be tracked when they leave the building, accompany residents, move equipment, or transfer to another assignment.

## Resident and Family Information
Staff assigned to resident movement should protect resident privacy while supporting continuity of care and appropriate updates to responsible parties. Detailed family notification and resident-location reporting are managed through the communications, resident information, and evacuation tracking tools.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Evacuation staff assignments come through command, operations, planning, or the labor pool.
- Clinical resident needs must guide who assists each resident.
- Residents should move with identification, needed records, medications, and essential belongings when possible.
- Staff accountability continues throughout evacuation and transport.
