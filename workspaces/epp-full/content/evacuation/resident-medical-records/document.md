# Resident Medical Records

## Purpose
This document explains how {{facility_name}} preserves and shares resident medical information during evacuation.

## Information Needed for Continuity of Care
Residents should move with or have access to enough medical information to support safe care at a holding area, stop-over point, receiving facility, or alternate care setting. Information may include face sheet or identifying information, diagnoses, allergies, code status, advance directives, responsible-party contacts, physician or provider contacts, current orders, medication information, recent notes, treatment needs, isolation status, diet or swallowing precautions, equipment needs, and special risks.

The facility's medical record process should reflect {{medical_records_system_description}} and the status of {{ehr_system_name}} during the incident.

## Record Preparation
Resident-care staff or assigned documentation staff should prepare paper or electronic record packets based on the time available, resident acuity, system access, and receiving destination. If the full record cannot travel with the resident, staff should send the minimum information necessary for continuity of care and document what was sent.

## Privacy and Information Sharing
Information should be shared with receiving providers, emergency responders, responsible parties, and other parties only as permitted by law and facility policy. During an emergency, staff should prioritize continuity of care while protecting resident privacy.

## Tracking
Staff should document whether the medical record, medication record, advance directives, and other key materials left with the resident, remained at the facility, or were transmitted another way. Receiving sites should be asked to confirm receipt when practical.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Residents need enough medical information to continue care after evacuation.
- If the full record cannot be sent, send the most critical care information.
- Privacy still matters during emergencies.
- Document what information was sent and where it went.
