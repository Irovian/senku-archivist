# Resident Preparation Guide - Independent Living Residents

## Purpose
This guide describes how {{facility_name}} prepares Independent Living residents for evacuation.

## Initial Assessment
Staff should determine which Independent Living residents can leave safely with responsible parties, which residents can self-evacuate with direction, and which residents need staff assistance, transportation, supervision, medical information, or temporary placement.

Residents should receive clear instructions about where to go, when to move, what to bring, and how updates will be provided. Staff should confirm whether a responsible party is involved and whether the resident's destination is known.

## Records and Identification
When an Independent Living resident is moving to another facility or care setting, staff should send available identification, emergency contact information, medication information, care needs, and any relevant medical information. Residents should have identification before leaving the building or stop-over area.

## Movement and Destination
Independent Living residents may use stairs, elevators, or other approved routes based on conditions. If elevators are cleared, staff should follow the plan for {{elevator_locations}}. If movement is delayed or external placement is not yet available, residents may be staged temporarily at {{temporary_stopover_location}} or another command-approved location.

## Belongings and Medications
Residents should take essential personal items, mobility aids, communication devices, chargers, medications, and assistive devices when time and safety allow. Staff should document destination, transportation, and responsible-party involvement.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Independent Living residents may still need tracking, instructions, and assistance.
- Responsible-party pickup must be documented.
- Residents should take essential items and medication information when possible.
- Destination and status updates should be communicated through the approved process.
