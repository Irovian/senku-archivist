# Care and Treatment of Evacuees

## Purpose
This document describes how {{facility_name}} maintains resident care during evacuation movement, holding, stop-over use, and transfer.

## Care Priorities
Resident safety, dignity, identification, supervision, infection prevention, medication continuity, hydration, toileting, nutrition, skin protection, behavioral support, and clinical monitoring should be maintained to the extent possible during evacuation.

Resident-care leaders should identify residents who need oxygen, suction, dialysis-related planning, wound care, behavioral support, mobility assistance, isolation precautions, or staff accompaniment. Portable oxygen may be obtained from {{portable_oxygen_supply_location}} and portable suction from {{portable_suction_location}} when those resources are needed and available.

## During Movement and Holding
Staff should use safe transfer techniques, resident-specific equipment, and appropriate personal protective equipment. Residents should be monitored for distress, pain, respiratory changes, confusion, elopement risk, falls risk, temperature exposure, dehydration, and fatigue.

Holding area staff should report clinical concerns to the assigned nurse, Operations Section Chief, or command center. Residents should not be left unattended in holding areas, vehicles, or receiving locations without an appropriate handoff.

## Handoff to Receiving Care
When residents transfer to another facility or care setting, staff should communicate the information needed for continuity of care, including medications, allergies, special precautions, equipment needs, and responsible-party information. Detailed records and resident tracking are managed through the related medical records and evacuation tracking tools.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Evacuation does not suspend resident-care responsibilities.
- Residents may need monitoring and reassessment during movement and holding.
- High-risk residents may need clinical staff accompaniment.
- Handoff information must travel with or be available for each resident.
