# Action Plan

## Purpose
This document describes the command-level action plan for initiating and managing a full building evacuation at {{facility_name}}.

## Initial Command Actions
When evacuation is being considered or ordered, the Incident Commander should activate the command center, confirm the primary location at {{primary_command_center_location}}, and use {{alternate_command_center_location}} if the primary location is unavailable.

The Incident Commander should announce the approved evacuation alert or plain-language direction using {{emergency_code_alert}} or the facility-approved evacuation code. The announcement should tell staff that evacuation preparation has begun, identify the affected area if known, and direct staff to follow assigned roles.

## Notifications
The command center should confirm that emergency services, emergency management, public health or regulatory contacts, and other required partners are notified when applicable. Notifications may be assigned to command staff, the Safety/Security/Liaison Officer, the Public Information Officer, or another designee.

The command center should also determine when residents, responsible parties, receiving partners, vendors, transportation providers, and staff families need updates. Information released externally should be coordinated so that resident location, condition, and privacy are protected.

## Command Coordination
The action plan should organize work through the incident command structure. Command should assign leaders for operations, planning, logistics, safety/security/liaison, public information, finance, documentation, labor pool, holding areas, and resident movement as needed for the incident.

The command center should request department status, resident census, staffing status, transportation needs, supply needs, and building-safety information. These reports support decisions about evacuation type, evacuation order, holding area readiness, transportation staging, and receiving-facility coordination.

## Documentation
The command center should document the evacuation decision, time of activation, major notifications, assigned command roles, department reports, resident movement decisions, outside-agency direction, and unresolved risks. Detailed tracking forms and action logs remain separate tools.

## What Staff Should Understand
- The action plan starts with command center activation and clear staff direction.
- The Incident Commander coordinates evacuation decisions with emergency services when available.
- Departments should report status and needs promptly.
- Forms, logs, and receiving-facility lists support the action plan but are maintained separately.
