# Resident Preparation Guide - Assisted Living Residents

## Purpose
This guide describes how {{facility_name}} prepares Assisted Living residents for evacuation.

## Resident Assessment
Assisted Living residents should be assessed for mobility, supervision needs, cognitive status, medication needs, assistive devices, dietary needs, communication needs, memory-care precautions, fall risk, behavioral support, and whether a responsible party is available.

Staff should identify residents who can leave with responsible parties, residents who need facility-arranged transportation, and residents who need placement in another healthcare or residential setting.

## Records, Identification, and Medications
Residents should be identified before movement and should have enough information to support continuity of care. When possible, send or make available resident identification, responsible-party information, medication list or medication administration information, current care needs, advance directives if applicable, allergies, provider contacts, and special precautions.

Medications, assistive devices, and essential supplies should be prepared according to resident need and available time. Controlled substances and medication records should be handled according to the separate medication procedure.

## Movement and Holding
Assisted Living residents should move using routes approved by command. If elevators are cleared, staff should follow {{elevator_locations}}; otherwise, use approved stairwell or alternate movement methods. Residents may be staged at holding areas or {{temporary_stopover_location}} while transportation and placement are coordinated.

Memory-care residents or residents at elopement risk may require separate space, additional staff, and closer supervision.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Assisted Living evacuation planning must account for care needs and supervision needs.
- Residents should move with identification, medication information, and essential belongings.
- Memory-care and elopement risks require extra controls.
- Responsible-party involvement and destination must be tracked.
