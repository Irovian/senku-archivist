# Activate Labor Pool

## Purpose
This document describes how {{facility_name}} activates and uses the labor pool during evacuation.

## Labor Pool Activation
The Planning Section Chief, Incident Commander, or designee should activate the labor pool when evacuation preparation or resident movement requires centralized staff assignment. Non-resident-care staff should report to the labor pool unless they are assigned to the command center, resident-care support, security, transportation, communications, or another specific response role.

The labor pool should be led by the Labor Pool Unit Leader or another assigned staff member. The leader should receive a briefing from command, confirm staffing needs, organize available personnel, and dispatch staff only when assignments are clear.

## Staff Call-Down and Reporting
Department heads may begin staff call-down or recall using {{staff_call_down_process}} when command determines that additional personnel are needed. Recalled staff should be told where to report, what identification or credentials to bring, and whether parking, access, or safety restrictions are in place.

Staff reporting to the labor pool should be logged, assigned, reassigned, and released in a way that allows command to account for personnel. Volunteer or temporary staff credentialing belongs in the related credentialing and incident command tools.

## Assignments
Labor pool assignments may include evacuation groups, equipment runners, resident escorts, holding area support, family area support, transportation staging support, documentation runners, supply movement, cleaning or equipment turnaround, and staff relief.

The Labor Pool Unit Leader should monitor fatigue, safety concerns, and skills needed for resident movement. Clinical tasks should be assigned only to staff with appropriate qualifications.

## What Staff Should Understand
- The labor pool is the central place for assigning available staff during evacuation.
- Staff should not self-deploy unless immediate life safety requires it.
- Staff call-down should use the approved facility process.
- Labor pool records support staff accountability and incident documentation.
