# Resident Personal Belongings

## Purpose
This document explains how {{facility_name}} manages essential resident belongings during evacuation.

## Essential Belongings
When time and safety allow, residents should evacuate with essential personal items that support identification, communication, comfort, dignity, and care. Items may include eyeglasses, hearing aids, dentures, mobility aids, prosthetics, clothing, shoes, communication devices, chargers, small comfort items, and labeled bags for personal effects.

Staff should prioritize items needed for resident safety and function over nonessential property. Large, fragile, high-value, or nonessential items may need to remain secured at the facility until recovery.

## Labeling and Tracking
Belongings that leave with residents should be labeled with the resident's name or approved identifier. Staff should document whether belongings went with the resident, remained in the room, were secured by the facility, or were released to a responsible party.

Valuables should be handled according to facility policy. If a responsible party takes valuables or belongings, staff should document the person, time, items, and contact information when practical.

## Handoff
Belongings should stay with the resident during movement when possible. If items are transported separately, staff should connect them to the resident's destination and notify the receiving location or responsible party.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Essential belongings support resident safety, communication, and dignity.
- Belongings should be labeled and tracked.
- Valuables require secure handling and documentation.
- Nonessential items may remain at the facility during urgent evacuation.
