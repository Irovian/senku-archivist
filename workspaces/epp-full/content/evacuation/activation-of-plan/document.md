# Activation of Plan

## Purpose
This document explains how {{facility_name}} activates the evacuation plan once leadership determines that full building evacuation may be needed.

## Activation Notice
The Incident Commander or designee should issue the evacuation activation notice using {{eop_activation_code_word}} or other facility-approved language. The notice should be clear enough for staff to understand whether they are preparing for possible evacuation, beginning staged movement, or starting immediate evacuation.

Resident-care staff, department managers, and supervisors should return to assigned areas unless directed otherwise. Staff without direct resident-care assignments should check in through the labor pool unless command has already assigned them to the command center, a department role, or another evacuation support function.

## Department Readiness
Departments should begin evacuation readiness activities immediately after activation. Each area should confirm resident census, staffing, equipment, transportation needs, resident preparation needs, and operational barriers. Departments should use the Department Rapid Assessment Form or equivalent process at {{department_rapid_assessment_form_location}}.

The command center should use department information to decide evacuation priority, holding area use, transportation needs, receiving-facility coordination, staff assignments, and whether additional staff call-down is needed through {{staff_call_down_process}}.

## Staff With Access or Mobility Needs
Staff who cannot safely report to the labor pool because elevators, corridors, or other routes are unavailable should remain in a safe location, notify their supervisor or command center, and follow evacuation directions for their area.

## Activation Boundaries
This document owns the evacuation-plan activation step. The evacuation order, prioritization, holding-area setup, transportation coordination, and resident preparation documents describe the next operational steps.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Activation means the facility is preparing for or beginning evacuation.
- Staff should report to assigned areas or the labor pool based on role.
- Departments must report census, staffing, equipment, and resident needs quickly.
- Staff who cannot reach the labor pool should communicate their location and await direction.
