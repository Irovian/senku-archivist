# Census Reduction Plan

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} may reduce census when emergency conditions require the facility to preserve capacity, prepare for evacuation, support surge, or maintain essential operations.

## Activation
The Incident Commander, Administrator, Director of Nursing, command center, or assigned leader should consider census reduction when staffing, supplies, utilities, access, resident care capacity, expected evacuation, incoming surge, or other emergency conditions make it necessary to safely reduce the number of residents on site.

Census reduction should be coordinated with resident care, discharge planning, transportation, communication, medical records, pharmacy, social services, and the applicable evacuation or surge procedure.

## Census Reduction Team
The command center may convene a census reduction team to recommend options and coordinate actions. The team may include nursing leadership, medical leadership, case management or social services, admissions, transportation or logistics, medical records, pharmacy, and other leaders needed for the event.

The team should identify residents who may be appropriate for temporary discharge, transfer, delayed admission, alternate placement, family-supported discharge, or another safe census-reduction option. Decisions should consider resident condition, physician orders, responsible-party availability, medication and treatment needs, home-care needs, transportation, equipment, infection prevention, and documentation requirements.

## Resident Discharge or Transfer Process
Before a resident leaves the facility under the census reduction plan, staff should coordinate the resident's care instructions, medication and treatment information, discharge or transfer orders when required, responsible-party communication, transportation arrangements, and receiving destination.

Residents waiting for family, transportation, transfer, or discharge should remain in a supervised holding or staging area when one is needed. Staff should continue care, monitoring, nutrition, hydration, medications, infection prevention, and safety checks until the resident leaves or is returned to normal placement.

## Communication
Residents and responsible parties should receive clear information about the reason for census reduction, the planned destination, transportation timing, care instructions, and whom to contact with questions.

If census reduction affects admissions, outside agencies, receiving providers, transportation partners, or emergency management partners, communication should be coordinated through the command center and approved communication procedures.

## Recovery
As conditions stabilize, the command center should reconcile residents discharged or transferred, confirm follow-up needs, document unresolved issues, evaluate whether deferred admissions may resume, and capture improvement items for after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Census reduction is used only when emergency conditions make it necessary to preserve safe operations or prepare for evacuation or surge.
- Resident discharge or transfer decisions require clinical, documentation, responsible-party, and transportation coordination.
- Residents waiting for discharge or transfer remain under facility care until handoff is complete.
- Communication with residents, families, receiving providers, and partners must be coordinated through approved channels.
- Detailed evacuation, transfer, medical-record, and surge steps remain in their dedicated documents.
