# Notification of Off-Duty Staff / Disaster Staffing Options

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} notifies off-duty staff, requests additional staffing support, and communicates disaster staffing options when normal staffing is not sufficient for emergency operations.

## Activation
The command center, staffing lead, or assigned leader should consider off-duty staff notification when the incident may extend beyond current staffing capacity, staff cannot safely leave, replacement staff may not arrive on time, resident care needs increase, evacuation or shelter-in-place is likely, or specialized support is needed.

Staffing coordination should occur through {{staffing_resource_owner_title}} or the current staffing lead. Staff contact information is maintained at {{employee_contact_information_location}}.

## Notification Process
Off-duty staff notification should use {{staff_call_down_process}}. The notification should identify the emergency status, requested role or shift, reporting location, travel safety considerations, callback instructions, and whether staff should wait for confirmation before reporting.

If the facility uses a recorded message line or internal staffing update line, the number is {{internal_recorded_message_phone_number}}.

Staff who cannot report should communicate their status through the process assigned by the staffing lead. The command center should track who was contacted, who responded, expected arrival times, declined assignments, and known barriers to reporting.

## Disaster Staffing Options
When additional support is needed, the facility may use {{disaster_staffing_options}}. Staffing options should be matched to resident needs, licensure requirements, supervision requirements, credentialing rules, and the duration of the event.

The command center should avoid over-calling staff without an assignment plan. Staff who report should receive clear duties, reporting lines, safety instructions, break expectations, and information about whether staff sheltering may be needed.

## Communication and Recovery
Staffing updates should be coordinated with department leaders so staff receive consistent instructions. If communications are disrupted, the facility should use the alternate communication methods described in the communication procedures.

After the event, the staffing lead should reconcile call-down records, staff hours, response barriers, agency or volunteer use, and improvement needs.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Off-duty staff should follow the facility's assigned call-down process.
- Reporting instructions should include where to go, what role is needed, and whether travel is safe.
- Staff who cannot report should communicate their status through the assigned process.
- Disaster staffing options must still respect role, licensure, credentialing, and supervision requirements.
- Management of Staff owns assignment decisions after staff are available.
