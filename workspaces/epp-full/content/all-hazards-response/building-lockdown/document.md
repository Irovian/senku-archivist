# Building Lockdown

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} restricts building access, monitors entry points, protects residents and staff, and coordinates communication during a lockdown or partial lockdown.

## Activation
The command center or assigned leader may initiate lockdown when a threat, security incident, civil disturbance, missing resident concern, external hazard, law enforcement request, severe weather condition, or other event requires controlled entry to the facility.

If the facility uses an alert or code to summon leadership or notify staff, the alert is {{emergency_code_alert}}. Staff should follow the announced instructions and report to assigned roles unless doing so would place residents or staff at immediate risk.

Lockdown should be coordinated with law enforcement, fire officials, emergency management, or other authorities when appropriate.

## Access Control
Entry points used during lockdown are {{lockdown_entry_points}}. Staff assigned to those locations should follow {{lockdown_access_screening_process}}.

The command center should assign monitoring roles using {{security_staffing_arrangements}}. Sensitive areas requiring monitoring include {{sensitive_area_monitoring_locations}}.

Doors may be locked or monitored to prevent unauthorized entry, but required emergency exits must remain usable for egress unless an authority having jurisdiction directs otherwise. Staff should not create a condition that traps residents, visitors, staff, or emergency responders inside the building.

## Resident and Staff Safety
Departments should account for residents, identify residents who need additional supervision, and report unsafe conditions to the command center. Staff should maintain care routines to the extent possible while limiting unnecessary movement.

Visitors, vendors, and nonessential movement may be restricted based on the type of incident. Staff at entry points should escalate aggressive behavior, suspicious activity, attempted unauthorized entry, or emergency access needs immediately.

If the lockdown is related to a weapon, active threat, missing resident, fire, severe weather, evacuation, or utility failure, the related hazard-specific procedure should also be activated.

## Communication and Recovery
The command center should communicate lockdown status, entry instructions, staffing needs, and all-clear information through the facility's approved communication channels.

Lockdown should end only when the command center or assigned leader determines that the access restriction is no longer needed, after consulting authorities when applicable. After termination, staff should restore normal access processes, document the event, report unresolved safety concerns, and support after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Lockdown controls entry; it must not block required emergency egress.
- Use only assigned entry points and screening processes during lockdown.
- Report unauthorized entry attempts, threats, blocked exits, and resident safety concerns immediately.
- Lockdown may operate alongside other hazard-specific procedures.
- All-clear communication comes from the command center or assigned leader.
