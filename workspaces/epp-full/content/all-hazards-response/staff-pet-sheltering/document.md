# Staff Pet Sheltering

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} may support limited staff pet sheltering when approved during extended emergency operations.

## Activation
Staff pet sheltering may be activated only at the discretion of the Incident Commander, Administrator, command center, or assigned leader. The decision should consider resident safety, allergies, infection prevention, available space, staffing continuity, sanitation, security, and whether pet sheltering would interfere with care or emergency operations.

Pet sheltering is optional staff support. It is not animal boarding, public animal sheltering, or a guarantee that pets can remain at the facility for the duration of an event.

## Registration and Accountability
When pet sheltering is approved, the command center should assign a shelter manager or designee to coordinate registration, accountability, and the assigned pet sheltering area.

Each pet should be registered with the responsible staff member, pet description, vaccination or license information when available, emergency contact information, arrival time, assigned location, and departure time. Pets should be checked in on arrival and checked out before leaving the assigned shelter area or facility.

The shelter manager should maintain a current list of sheltered pets and responsible staff members so the command center can reconcile pets during the incident and at demobilization.

## Shelter Area Operations
Pets should be housed only in the assigned pet sheltering area and should be separated from residents, resident care areas, food service areas, and staff or family sleeping areas unless the command center authorizes a specific exception.

Pet owners remain responsible for the care, feeding, medication, restraint, hygiene, and behavior of their pets. Pets should be kept in carriers, cages, kennels, or on appropriate restraint unless the shelter manager directs otherwise.

The facility may limit eligible pets based on safety, sanitation, space, species, behavior, vaccination status, local requirements, or incident conditions.

## Pet Supplies
Staff should bring the supplies needed to care for their pets. Supplies may include a carrier or kennel, leash or restraint, food, water, bowls, medications, medical or vaccination records, a current pet photo, cleaning supplies, waste bags or litter supplies, bedding, and emergency contact information.

The command center may deny or end pet sheltering when supplies are inadequate, sanitation cannot be maintained, a pet creates a safety risk, or conditions change.

## Recovery
When staff pet sheltering is no longer needed, the shelter manager should reconcile the pet roster, confirm pets have checked out, arrange cleaning and sanitation of the assigned area, report safety or sanitation issues, and provide improvement items for after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Staff pet sheltering requires command approval.
- Pets may stay only in assigned pet sheltering areas.
- Staff remain responsible for pet supplies, care, restraint, medications, and cleanup.
- Pet sheltering must not interfere with resident care, infection prevention, life safety, or staff rest areas.
- The command center may restrict, deny, or end pet sheltering if conditions require it.
