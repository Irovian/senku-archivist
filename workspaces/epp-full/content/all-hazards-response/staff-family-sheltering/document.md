# Staff Family Sheltering

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} may support sheltering of staff family members when approved during extended emergency operations.

## Activation
Staff family sheltering may be activated only at the discretion of the Incident Commander, Administrator, command center, or assigned leader. The decision should consider resident safety, staffing continuity, available space, security, infection prevention, utilities, food and water support, and whether sheltering family members would interfere with resident care.

Staff family sheltering is optional facility support for workforce continuity. It is not a public shelter, resident-family shelter, pet shelter, or guarantee that family members can remain at the facility for the duration of an event.

## Registration and Assignment
When staff family sheltering is approved, the command center should assign a shelter manager or designee to coordinate registration, accountability, and shelter-area assignment.

Each sheltered family member should be registered on the facility's assigned information sheet or equivalent record. Registration should include identifying information, the staff member responsible for the family member, emergency contact information, assigned shelter area, arrival time, and departure time.

Sheltered family members should sign in on arrival and check out before leaving the assigned shelter area or facility. The shelter manager should maintain a current roster so the command center can account for all sheltered family members during the incident and at demobilization.

## Shelter Area Operations
The command center should assign shelter areas that do not interfere with resident care, emergency egress, clinical operations, infection prevention requirements, or staff work areas. A temporary holding area may be used while shelter areas are being prepared.

Family members should receive instructions on assigned areas, off-limit areas, restroom access, safety expectations, meal and hydration arrangements, emergency alerts, and how to contact the shelter manager. Family members should not enter resident care areas unless specifically authorized.

If family members are physically able and the facility requests assistance, they may be asked to help with non-clinical tasks that do not require licensure, resident-care training, access to protected information, or unsafe work.

## Personal Supplies
Staff should be told what family members may bring and what is prohibited. Approved items may include bedding, toiletries, clothing, medications, nonperishable food, water, flashlights, phone chargers, and emergency contact information. Prohibited items should include weapons, alcohol, flame-producing items, hazardous materials, and items that create electrical, safety, sanitation, or storage concerns.

The command center may restrict belongings when space, infection control, security, or life-safety conditions require it.

## Recovery
When staff family sheltering is no longer needed, the shelter manager should reconcile the roster, confirm family members have checked out, arrange cleaning and reset of shelter areas, report any safety or support concerns, and provide improvement items for after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Staff family sheltering requires command approval.
- Family sheltering must not interfere with resident care, life safety, or emergency operations.
- Sheltered family members must register, remain in assigned areas, and check out before leaving.
- Pet sheltering and public sheltering are separate decisions and procedures.
- The command center may limit or end staff family sheltering if conditions change.
