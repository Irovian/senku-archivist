# Loss of Parking / Inability to Commute to Facility

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} manages staff reporting, alternate parking, transportation support, and communication when parking access or travel to the facility is disrupted.

## Activation
The command center, Incident Commander, Administrator, staffing lead, or assigned leader should consider this procedure when parking areas are unavailable, roads are blocked, law enforcement restricts access, severe weather affects travel, utility or structural hazards affect parking areas, or staff cannot safely commute to the facility.

This procedure should be coordinated with staffing, communication, security, maintenance, transportation, and any applicable hazard-specific response.

## Staff Reporting Expectations
Staff who are asked to report during disrupted travel conditions should use safe routes, carry facility identification when available, follow law enforcement instructions, and avoid placing themselves at unreasonable risk.

Staff who cannot safely reach the facility should notify their supervisor, staffing lead, or assigned call-down contact as soon as possible. The command center should track staff unable to report, staff delayed in transit, and staff who may need transportation assistance.

## Alternate Parking and Transportation
The command center should identify whether alternate parking, shuttle service, facility vehicles, outside transportation providers, or staged pickup times are needed.

When alternate parking is used, staff should receive clear instructions about the location, route, pickup point, pickup schedule, identification expectations, personal safety, and whom to contact if they cannot reach the location.

Maintenance, security, logistics, or assigned staff should secure unsafe parking areas, reroute traffic when possible, post signs, preserve emergency vehicle access, and notify vendors, emergency responders, and other affected parties of changed access routes.

## Communication
Parking and commute updates should be communicated through approved staff communication methods. Updates should include the nature of the disruption, expected duration when known, alternate parking or transportation instructions, reporting expectations, safety reminders, and instructions for staff who cannot report.

If communication systems are disrupted, the facility should use the alternate methods described in the internal and external communication procedures.

## Recovery
When normal parking or commute access is restored, the command center should notify staff, remove temporary signage, close alternate parking or transportation arrangements, reconcile staff reporting records, document costs, and capture improvement items for after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Do not endanger yourself while attempting to report to the facility.
- Carry facility identification when reporting during restricted travel conditions.
- Follow assigned call-down, parking, transportation, and reporting instructions.
- Notify the staffing lead or supervisor if you cannot safely report.
- Keep emergency vehicle access, unsafe parking areas, and temporary traffic routes clear.
