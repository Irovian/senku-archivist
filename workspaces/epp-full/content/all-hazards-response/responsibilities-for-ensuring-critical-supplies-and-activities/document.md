# Responsibilities for Ensuring Critical Supplies and Activities

## Purpose
This document identifies the facility roles and departments responsible for monitoring, maintaining, replenishing, and escalating critical supplies and activities during a disaster.

## Responsibility Framework
Departments should monitor assigned critical supplies during normal operations and during emergencies. When supplies are low, delivery is disrupted, or a foreseeable emergency warrants inventory buildup, responsible departments should notify the command center or assigned leader and begin replenishment, conservation, or contingency actions.

During emergency activation, these responsibilities may be coordinated through the Incident Command System, the command center, or another assigned leadership structure. During a {{emergency_code_alert}} activation, supply responsibilities should align with the applicable incident command assignments.

## Clinical and Resident Care Resources
{{clinical_resource_owner_titles}} are responsible for clinical and resident-care resource coordination, including:

- nursing staffing needs that affect resident care;
- resident assessment, scheduling, admissions, discharge, and service modification issues;
- medication and pharmaceutical supply concerns;
- resident information needed for care continuity;
- coordination with dining, activities, and other departments when resident services must be modified.

## Staffing Resources
{{staffing_resource_owner_title}} is responsible for staffing resource coordination, including staffing needs for affected departments, call-back support, staff availability information, and coordination with the command center when staffing levels may limit essential services.

## Maintenance and Utility Support Resources
{{maintenance_resource_owner_title}} is responsible for facility support resources, including:

- normal and emergency water support;
- generator fuel and emergency power support;
- heating, cooling, mechanical, and facility systems;
- {{boiler_fuel_responsibility_description}};
- portable generator or temporary power support when applicable;
- coordination with utility, fuel, repair, and facility service vendors.

Detailed generator, utility, fuel, and load-shedding procedures belong in Managing Utilities During a Disaster and the related utility-specific procedures.

## Purchasing and Supply Replenishment
{{purchasing_resource_owner_title}} is responsible for purchasing and supply replenishment coordination, including medical supplies, personal protective equipment, paper supplies, portable oxygen cylinders, vendor communication, emergency purchasing, and alternate source coordination when normal vendors cannot meet facility needs.

## Dining and Food Service Resources
{{dining_resource_owner_title}} is responsible for food and dining-service resources, including potable water used for dining support, perishable food, dry stock, emergency menus, disposable dining supplies, and coordination with vendors or alternate sources when normal delivery is disrupted.

## Environmental and Housekeeping Resources
{{environmental_resource_owner_title}} is responsible for environmental service resources, including linen, housekeeping supplies, regulated medical waste support, bedding supplies, sanitation supplies, and coordination with waste, laundry, and environmental service vendors.

## Escalation and Documentation
Responsible departments should escalate shortages, damaged supplies, vendor delays, staff limitations, or service constraints that may affect resident care or essential operations. Escalation should occur through the command center or assigned leadership structure.

Documentation may include inventory records, department reports, purchase requests, vendor notes, resource requests, supply substitutions, conservation measures, and recovery cost records.

- Baseline Assessment of 96 Hr. Capabilities, which provides the baseline resource snapshot.

- Disaster staffing documents, which own detailed staff notification, sheltering, and staffing options.
- Section F resource lists, vendor lists, and department inventories, which maintain detailed contacts, quantities, locations, and vendor arrangements.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Each department is responsible for monitoring the critical supplies and activities assigned to it.
- Department titles may differ by facility; responsibility must follow the function even when the local title changes.
- Shortages and supply-chain concerns should be reported early.
- During activation, supply responsibilities may shift into command-center or incident command assignments.
- Detailed quantities, vendor contacts, and storage locations should remain in resource lists and department inventories.
