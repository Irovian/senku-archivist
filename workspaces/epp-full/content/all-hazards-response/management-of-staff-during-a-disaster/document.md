# Management of Staff During a Disaster

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} assesses staffing needs, assigns available staff, supports essential operations, and adjusts staffing priorities during emergency operations.

## Staffing Coordination
The command center or assigned leader should coordinate staffing through {{staffing_resource_owner_title}} or the current staffing lead. Staff contact information is maintained at {{employee_contact_information_location}}.

Staffing decisions should consider resident acuity, required services, incident duration, department staffing levels, staff fatigue, transportation barriers, sheltering needs, communication outages, and whether additional staff must be called in.

Department leaders should report available staff, critical staffing gaps, staff unable to leave, staff unable to report, and immediate support needs to the command center. Department assessments should use {{department_rapid_assessment_form_location}} or the facility's current reporting process.

## Assignment and Reassignment
The command center should prioritize staffing for resident care, medication administration, food and hydration support, life safety, security, utilities, communication, documentation, and essential environmental services.

Staff may be reassigned to support emergency duties within their training, licensure, role, and facility policy. Disaster staffing options include {{disaster_staffing_options}}.

If additional staffing is required, the facility should use {{staff_call_down_process}} or the current off-duty notification procedure. Volunteers, agency staff, contracted staff, or partner resources should be managed according to facility credentialing, supervision, and documentation procedures.

## Staff Safety and Support
Leaders should monitor staff fatigue, breaks, meals, hydration, medication needs, emotional stress, and ability to safely continue working. Staff who are unable to leave because of road conditions, shelter-in-place, curfew, or other hazards should be referred to the Staff Sheltering procedure.

Staff should receive situation updates, assignment changes, safety instructions, and reporting expectations through approved communication channels.

## Recovery
As the incident stabilizes, leaders should transition from emergency staffing to normal assignments, document staffing gaps and extraordinary hours, identify follow-up support needs, and capture improvement opportunities for after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Staffing assignments are coordinated through the command center or assigned staffing lead.
- Staff may be reassigned to emergency duties within role, training, licensure, and policy limits.
- Report staffing gaps, fatigue, transportation barriers, and inability to leave or report.
- Off-duty notification is handled through the staff call-down process.
- Staff sheltering logistics belong in the Staff Sheltering procedure.
