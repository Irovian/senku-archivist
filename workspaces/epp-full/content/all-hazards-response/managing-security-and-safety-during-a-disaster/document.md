# Managing Security and Safety During a Disaster

## Purpose
This procedure describes how {{facility_name}} manages security, access control, safety monitoring, and staff assignment during emergency operations when normal building movement, visitor access, supply movement, or resident safety controls may be disrupted.

## Activation and Coordination
The command center or assigned leader should evaluate security and safety needs whenever the Emergency Operations Plan is activated, a hazard affects building access, residents or staff may be at risk, utilities or communications are disrupted, or outside conditions require additional monitoring.

When security support is needed, the command center should assign staff according to {{security_staffing_arrangements}} and coordinate those assignments with department leaders. If the facility uses a disaster alert or code to summon leadership or support staff, the alert is {{emergency_code_alert}}.

Department leaders should assess their assigned areas and report security, staffing, access, resident-safety, or resource concerns to the command center. Assessment documentation should use {{department_rapid_assessment_form_location}} or the facility's current rapid assessment process.

## Security and Safety Priorities
Security and safety actions should support resident protection, staff safety, emergency access, communication, and continuity of care. Assigned staff should monitor entrances, exits, exterior doors, parking or delivery areas, high-risk resident areas, supply storage, medication areas, oxygen or medical gas storage, generator areas, and other sensitive locations identified by {{sensitive_area_monitoring_locations}}.

The command center should determine whether the incident requires visitor restriction, entry screening, vendor control, additional rounding, resident relocation within the building, emergency communication with law enforcement, or activation of Building Lockdown or another hazard-specific procedure.

Security measures must not block required emergency egress, delay fire response, prevent evacuation when ordered, or interfere with emergency responders.

## Communication and Escalation
Security or safety concerns should be reported immediately to the command center or assigned leader. Urgent threats, violence, suspicious activity, blocked exits, missing residents, fire or smoke, medical emergencies, and unsafe crowding should be escalated using the appropriate emergency code, emergency services call, or hazard-specific procedure.

If additional leadership or external coordination is required, escalation should follow {{primary_emergency_contact_name}} or the current emergency contact process.

## Recovery
When the security or safety concern stabilizes, the command center should release extra monitoring assignments, restore normal access processes, communicate changes to affected departments, collect documentation, and capture improvement needs for after-action review.

## What Staff Should Understand
- Security and safety assignments are coordinated through the command center.
- Monitoring sensitive areas does not replace resident care duties or emergency response.
- Security actions must preserve required egress and emergency responder access.
- Building Lockdown owns detailed lockdown steps when access restriction is required.
- Report threats, unsafe conditions, blocked exits, and suspicious activity immediately.
